AI Marketing & Automation

AI can reduce repetitive work when it has a clear purpose. We plan AI marketing around a specific workflow, available information and human oversight.

Discuss your project ↗

What does the service include?

The first step is defining the task: drafting content, qualifying enquiries, answering routine questions or working with internal information. We examine the workflow, data sources and situations that require a handoff to a team member.

AI-generated content needs editorial review and a defined brand voice. Sales and HR assistants need boundaries, approved information and clear points of human involvement. Integration options depend on the capabilities of existing systems and the agreed conditions for accessing data.

  • Workflow review and automation opportunities
  • Requirements and scenarios for content or an assistant
  • Assessment of integrations with existing systems
  • Test cases, review procedures and usage guidance

Who is it for?

For teams handling recurring questions, repetitive content work or a high volume of initial enquiries. A focused pilot can help assess practical value before extending the solution.

How we work

  1. Understand the brief and audience
  2. Agree the scope and work plan
  3. Develop and implement the solution
  4. Review results and refine the work

Questions about this service

Does AI replace the team?

The aim is to support repetitive tasks. Important decisions, uncertain situations and quality control require planned human involvement.

Which systems can be integrated?

This depends on API capabilities, access permissions and the project requirements. We assess the existing setup before agreeing the integration scope.

How are AI responses checked?

Common and difficult scenarios are tested before launch. Response boundaries, editorial review and handoff procedures are agreed as part of the workflow.

Preparing for an introductory call

Tell us about your business, product, audience, current channels and the problem you want to solve. Links to your website or social profiles help us understand the starting point. We can then discuss the scope, priorities and budget together.
