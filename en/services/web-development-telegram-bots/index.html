Web Development, Apps & Telegram Bots

A digital product should help its users complete a real task. That task is the starting point for planning a website, app or bot.

Discuss your project ↗

What does the service include?

Website development starts with the page structure, content, user journey and key actions. Mobile layouts, loading performance, technical SEO foundations and working forms are considered as part of the product. Content volume and language requirements influence the scope.

For Telegram bots, mobile apps and AI applications, we define scenarios, user roles, data flows and integrations. Core journeys are checked before launch. Domains, hosting, third-party services and ongoing support are clarified separately in the proposal so that operational responsibilities are understood.

  • Page structure or application scenarios and requirements
  • User interface and responsive layouts
  • Agreed features and system integrations
  • Core journey checks, launch and project handover

Who is it for?

For businesses that need to present their services online, collect enquiries or organise customer interactions through a website, app or bot.

How we work

  1. Understand the brief and audience
  2. Agree the scope and work plan
  3. Develop and implement the solution
  4. Review results and refine the work

Questions about this service

What types of websites do you build?

Depending on the brief, the project may be a landing page, company website or custom web product. Features and content requirements are discussed at the start.

Are domains and hosting included?

Domain registration, hosting and external service costs are listed separately in the proposal. Ownership and access arrangements are also agreed.

Does development include SEO?

The agreed scope can include page structure, metadata, mobile layouts and technical indexing foundations. Search positions also depend on content, competition and ongoing site development.

Preparing for an introductory call

Tell us about your business, product, audience, current channels and the problem you want to solve. Links to your website or social profiles help us understand the starting point. We can then discuss the scope, priorities and budget together.
