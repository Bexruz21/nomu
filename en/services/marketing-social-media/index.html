Marketing, Social Media & Paid Advertising

Marketing starts with understanding who to reach, what to offer and which channels to use. NOMU connects strategy, social media and paid advertising to your business goals.

Discuss your project ↗

What does the service include?

Strategy work examines your product, audience, competitors and customer journey. When appropriate, customer development interviews help clarify buyer needs and decision-making. These findings inform the audiences, messages and channels to prioritise.

Social media management builds regular communication through content themes, publishing plans, copy and visual assets. Paid social and performance campaigns test audiences, offers and creative approaches. We review agreed metrics and use the findings to guide subsequent activity, rather than treating each campaign as an isolated launch.

  • Audience, competitor and existing channel analysis
  • Marketing direction, content themes and publishing plan
  • Copy, design and video content briefs
  • Campaign planning, creative tests and progress reports

Who is it for?

For businesses launching a product, building a more consistent social media presence or seeking a clearer view of advertising leads. An engagement can focus on one channel or combine several workstreams.

How we work

  1. Understand the brief and audience
  2. Agree the scope and work plan
  3. Develop and implement the solution
  4. Review results and refine the work

Questions about this service

How does social media management differ from paid advertising?

Social media management develops ongoing communication through your profile and content. Paid advertising places sponsored messages in front of selected audiences. They can work together, with separate responsibilities and budgets.

Is advertising spend included in the fee?

Platform advertising spend and agency fees are clarified separately in the proposal. Costs are agreed before a campaign starts.

How is performance evaluated?

Depending on the goal, we track reach, enquiries, lead quality and advertising costs. Sales also depend on the product, offer and sales process. Relevant metrics are agreed at the start.

Preparing for an introductory call

Tell us about your business, product, audience, current channels and the problem you want to solve. Links to your website or social profiles help us understand the starting point. We can then discuss the scope, priorities and budget together.
