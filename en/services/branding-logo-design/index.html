Branding, Naming & Logo Design

A brand is more than its logo. Its name, visual identity and communication style work together to make the business recognisable.

Discuss your project ↗

What does the service include?

Branding begins by clarifying your offer, audience and differences from competitors. Naming work establishes selection criteria and develops options. Before choosing a name, responsibilities for language, domain and legal checks should be agreed.

Logo design is developed alongside colour, typography and supporting visual elements. Packaging takes the product type, information hierarchy and production requirements into account. Final assets are delivered in agreed formats with guidance for consistent use across the chosen applications.

  • Brand objectives, audience and visual direction
  • Name options and evaluation criteria when naming is in scope
  • Logo, colour and typography system
  • Agreed applications, packaging or usage guidelines

Who is it for?

For businesses launching a company or product, refreshing an existing identity or bringing different channels into a consistent visual system.

How we work

  1. Understand the brief and audience
  2. Agree the scope and work plan
  3. Develop and implement the solution
  4. Review results and refine the work

Questions about this service

Can I commission only a logo?

Yes. The scope can cover a logo, visual identity or a wider engagement including naming and packaging. We agree the deliverables before starting.

How long does branding take?

A visual identity project typically takes 4–8 weeks. Timing depends on the scope, options, approval rounds and number of deliverables.

Which files are delivered?

Required vector and digital formats are agreed at the start. Print production requirements are clarified separately for relevant materials.

Preparing for an introductory call

Tell us about your business, product, audience, current channels and the problem you want to solve. Links to your website or social profiles help us understand the starting point. We can then discuss the scope, priorities and budget together.
